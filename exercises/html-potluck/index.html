Friday Night Movie Showcase 

Ready Or Not


 2019   Director(s): Matt Bettinelli-Olpin and Tyler Gillett

GENRE: Horror/DARK COMEDY     RUNTIME: 1HR 35MIN     RATING: R

SYNOPSIS

A new bride whose wedding night in an opulent family mansion turns into a mysterious initiation game steeped in tradition. Beneath its elegant setting and quirky relatives lies growing tension as the ritual reveals ominous undertones. Worth watching for Samara Weaving's charismatic lead performance, sharp mix of suspense and dark comedy, and biting commentary on privilege; praised for its tight pacing and stylish visuals.

The Fog


 1980   Director: John Carpenter

GENRE: Suspense/Horror     RUNTIME: 1HR 29MIN      RATING: R

SYNOPSIS

A coastal town prepares for its centennial as a mysterious glowing fog descends, bringing supernatural forces tied to dark secrets from its founding. Its atmospheric tension, eerie visuals, and memorable performances from Jamie Lee Curtis and Adrienne Barbeau make it appealing for fans of suspenseful horror. Reviews note stylish direction and effective scares, though some critique its plot logic. It has since gained cult status.

Doctor Sleep


 2019   Director: Mike Flanagan

GENRE: Horror     RUNTIME: 2HR 32MIN      RATING: R

SYNOPSIS

Dan Torrance (Ewan McGregor), still haunted by his childhood and gifted with psychic abilities, meets Abra Stone, a young prodigy with similar powers. Together they face the sinister True Knot , a cult feeding on psychic children. Worth watching for its strong performances, rich atmosphere, and thoughtful connection to The Shining, though its slower pace offers more emotional depth than traditional scares.
